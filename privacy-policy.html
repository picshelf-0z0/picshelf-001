1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how PicShelf ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information in full compliance with Google Play Store policies. Our core principle is simple: we do not collect your personal data, and all of your data stays on your own device.

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not ask for, collect, transmit, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or personal identity.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track, collect, or transmit your physical or GPS location to any external server.
  • 100% On-Device Storage: All of your photos, albums, tags, edits, and app data live exclusively on your own device.
  • Permanent Erasure on Uninstall: If you delete or uninstall the app, all data stored inside the app is permanently erased.
  • 18+ Target Audience: This application is intended exclusively for users aged 18 years and older (18+).

2. Device Permissions & Purpose

To provide offline photo organizing, AI subject isolation, duplicate cleanup, and geotagging features, PicShelf asks for the following Android system permissions. Every permission is used strictly locally on your device:

Permission Android Identifier Purpose in PicShelf
Photos & Media READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Enables browsing your gallery, displaying photos, organizing albums, and performing on-device image editing locally on your phone.
Photo Location Metadata ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION Enables reading EXIF GPS tags embedded in your existing photos strictly on-device so you can view capture locations on the built-in world map. Location data is never sent off your device.
Camera CAMERA Enables taking new photos directly into your organized albums on your device.
GPS Location (Optional) ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Allows embedding GPS coordinates locally into the EXIF metadata of photos taken with the in-app camera. Can be toggled on or off at any time in Settings. Never transmitted to any server.
Google Play Billing com.android.vending.BILLING Enables secure subscription purchases managed directly by the Google Play Store.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays local background progress bars during batch album exports, duplicate scans, and optional Google Play update checks.

3. Data Collection, Storage & Processing

A. Zero Collection of Personal Data, Email, or Location

PicShelf does not require user registration or login. We do not collect, harvest, upload, or store your email address, name, phone number, addressbook contacts, or device location. Any location coordinates accessed via optional camera geotagging or photo EXIF metadata are processed 100% locally on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

B. 100% On-Device Media Processing

All AI neural networks (subject isolation, background blur, object removal), image adjustments (filters, curves, DPI resizing), and album duplicate detectors run strictly inside the local sandbox on your phone. Your photos, videos, audio, and albums are never uploaded to any cloud server.

C. Biometric & Passcode Security

If you enable App Lock or Biometric Lock, verification is performed locally using Android's hardware security framework (BiometricPrompt / KeyStore). Biometric data never leaves your hardware security module.

D. Advertising and Analytics

PicShelf contains no advertising SDKs or tracking cookies and does not track how you browse or organize your media. Purchase and subscription analytics used strictly for Google Play billing verification are described below. We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your personal information or media.

4. In-App Purchases & Payments

All financial transactions for PicShelf Pro are handled exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. We never view, collect, or store your email address, credit card, debit card, bank account, or UPI details.

All billing terms, renewals, and refunds are governed by Google's official policies. You can cancel or manage subscriptions at any time via the Google Play Store app > Payments & Subscriptions.

PicShelf uses RevenueCat to verify and restore Pro access. Its SDK sends Google Play purchase tokens, purchase and subscription information, an anonymous app user identifier, and necessary app/device technical information to RevenueCat over HTTPS. RevenueCat retains purchase and entitlement records for subscription management and purchase analytics. Your photos, videos, audio, email, location, and album organization are not sent to RevenueCat. Clearing local app data does not delete purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights & Permanent Data Erasure on Uninstall

All of your media organization, in-app albums, tags, notes, presets, and app preferences are stored exclusively on your own device (in local app storage and your phone's media gallery). We do not maintain any external servers or cloud backups of your data:

  • Deleting Items in the App: You can delete any photo, video, or album directly inside PicShelf at any time.
  • Permanent Data Erasure When Uninstalling / Deleting the App: If you uninstall (delete) the PicShelf app from your device—or clear its storage via Settings > Apps > PicShelf > Storage > Clear Data—everything stored inside the app (including all in-app albums, vault items, tags, edits, presets, and local databases) is permanently and irreversibly erased. Because all user data lives strictly on your own device and never on our servers, we cannot recover your in-app data once you delete the app.

  • This application is designed for adults (18+) and is not directed at, marketed to, or intended for children or anyone under the age of 18 (including children under 13 under COPPA).
  • If you are under 18 years old, please do not install or use PicShelf.
  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store any personal data, email, or location information from anyone of any age, including anyone under 18.
